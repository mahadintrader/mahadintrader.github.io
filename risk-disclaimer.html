Mahadin Trader is a personal website documenting Mahmudul Hasan's learning journey in Forex trading. Articles, notes, journal ideas, market observations, and other materials are shared for educational and informational purposes only.

Not Financial Advice

Nothing on this website should be understood as financial, investment, legal, tax, or professional advice. No information on this website is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any currency or financial instrument.

Trading Risk

The Forex market can move quickly and unexpectedly. Leverage can increase both potential gains and potential losses. You should understand the risks involved before trading and should never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

No Profit Guarantee

Mahadin Trader does not promise, guarantee, or represent that any particular trading result or level of profit will be achieved. Losses are a normal risk of trading and individual results can vary significantly.

Risk Management

As part of the personal trading discipline documented on this website, the approach emphasizes protecting capital before pursuing profit. Personal rules include controlled risk per trade, use of stop loss, avoiding overtrading, and stopping after a series of losses.

Do Your Own Research

Before making any trading or investment decision, conduct your own research and consider seeking advice from a qualified financial professional who can assess your individual circumstances.

Personal Trading Discipline

The trading discipline principles used in this personal journey include:

  • Follow a trading plan rather than chasing profit.
  • Avoid revenge trading, impulsive entries, and emotional decisions.
  • Use appropriate stop loss and risk controls.
  • Check market and economic-news conditions before trading.
  • Keep a trading journal and review mistakes and losing trades.
  • Focus on consistency, patience, discipline, and continuous improvement.
