Trading Journal

A personal space to record market observations, trading decisions, mistakes, lessons and improvements throughout my journey.

Why I Keep a Trading Journal

A trading journal helps me look back at my decisions instead of relying only on memory. By recording what I see, why I make a decision and what I learn afterward, I can gradually identify mistakes, improve my process and develop better trading habits.

What I Record

These are the main areas I want to document during my trading and learning process.

01

Market Observation

Notes about market structure, price movement, important levels and overall market conditions.

02

Trade Idea

The reason behind a potential setup and the conditions I am watching before taking any decision.

03

Risk Plan

Notes about risk, position sizing and the level of risk involved in a potential trade.

04

Execution

Recording what actually happened when a trade or practice decision was executed.

05

Emotions

Observations about patience, fear, greed, hesitation or other emotions that may influence decisions.

06

Lessons Learned

The most important lessons from each review and what I can do differently next time.

Journal Entry Format

A simple structure I can use when documenting a future trade or market observation.

Future Trade / Market Review

Template for recording a complete trading observation.

Date & Time Record when the observation or trade took place.
Currency Pair Record the market or currency pair being analyzed.
Market Condition Trend, range, structure and important price areas.
Setup / Reason Explain what created interest in the potential setup.
Risk Management Record planned risk and position management.
Result Record the outcome and what actually happened.

Review Process

The purpose of the journal is not simply to record trades. It is to learn from them.

01

What Did I See?

Review the market structure, price action and conditions that were visible at the time.

02

Why Did I Make the Decision?

Check whether the decision followed a clear plan or was influenced by emotion or impulse.

03

What Went Right or Wrong?

Identify the strengths and weaknesses of the analysis and execution.

04

What Did I Learn?

Write down the main lesson that can help improve future analysis and decision-making.

05

What Can I Improve?

Choose one or more specific areas to work on during the next stage of the journey.

Journal Mindset
