Trading Journal

Learn From Every Trade.

This page is designed to document my trading decisions, observations, mistakes, and lessons. The purpose of the journal is not simply to count wins and losses, but to understand the process behind each decision.

My Journal Philosophy

A loss can be part of learning. A good trade can still lose, and a bad trade can still win. I want to judge my progress by process, discipline, risk control, and the lessons I take from each session.

Process • Review • Improve
01 — What I Record

My Trading Journal

Each journal entry can capture the context around a trade so I can review what happened and why.

01

Market Context

Record the market condition, trend, structure, key levels, and relevant observations before entering.

02

Trade Setup

Document the setup or idea that led to the trade and whether the planned conditions were actually confirmed.

03

Entry & Exit

Record entry, stop-loss, take-profit, exit, and the reason behind the decision.

04

Risk & Reward

Track planned risk and reward so that risk management remains part of the decision-making process.

05

Emotion & Psychology

Note fear, greed, impatience, hesitation, overconfidence, or other emotions that may have influenced the trade.

06

Lesson Learned

Write down what worked, what failed, what mistake was made, and what should be improved next time.

02 — Journal Template

Trade Entry Structure

A simple template I can use later when I start publishing individual journal records.

DateTrading session date
Pair / InstrumentExample: EUR/USD
Market BiasWhat I observed before the trade
SetupWhy the setup was considered valid
EntryEntry level and confirmation
Stop-LossPlanned invalidation / protection level
Take-ProfitPlanned target and risk/reward
ResultOutcome and what happened

Review Notes

  • Did I follow my trading plan?
  • Was the entry confirmed or impulsive?
  • Was risk controlled properly?
  • Did news or market conditions affect the trade?
  • What mistake or lesson should I remember?
03 — Performance Review

Review Before I Improve

Regular review helps turn individual trades into useful feedback for future learning.

Daily Review

  • Did I follow my plan?
  • Did I avoid overtrading?
  • Did I control emotions?
  • Did I respect stop-loss and risk rules?
  • What is one lesson from today?

Weekly Review

  • Review winning and losing trades.
  • Identify repeated mistakes.
  • Look for patterns in decision-making.
  • Review strategy and execution quality.
  • Choose one or two areas to improve next week.
Important: This journal is intended as a personal learning and record-keeping system. It does not represent verified trading performance, guaranteed returns, or financial advice.
