My Trading Goals

Building a Trader, Not Chasing a Trade.

My goals are centered on learning, discipline, risk management, patience, and continuous improvement. I am still learning Forex trading, so the focus is on building a strong foundation step by step.

“Follow the plan. Protect the capital. Keep improving.”
FocusKnowledge & discipline
ApproachStep-by-step learning
PriorityRisk management
MindsetConsistency over emotion
01 — Short Term

Short-Term Goals

My immediate goals are about creating a reliable learning and practice process rather than chasing quick profits.

01

Strengthen the Foundation

Build a clearer understanding of Price Action, Technical Analysis, Fundamental Analysis, Supply & Demand, SMC, ICT, and other core concepts.

02

Build a Trading Plan

Develop a structured plan covering setups, trend confirmation, entries, risk/reward, news awareness, and rules for avoiding impulsive trades.

03

Improve Risk Management

Practice protecting capital with controlled risk, proper stop-loss use, and a clear understanding that capital protection comes first.

04

Develop Trading Psychology

Work on patience, emotional control, discipline, and avoiding revenge trading or decisions driven by fear and greed.

05

Maintain a Trading Journal

Record trades, mistakes, observations, and lessons so that each trading session becomes an opportunity to learn.

06

Review & Improve

Regularly review performance and losing trades, identify recurring mistakes, and improve the process over time.

02 — Long Term

Long-Term Goals

The long-term objective is not to claim perfection, but to gradually become a more knowledgeable, disciplined, and consistent trader.

A

Become a Disciplined Trader

Follow a defined process and trading rules instead of allowing emotions to control decisions.

B

Build Consistency

Focus on quality over quantity and develop repeatable habits through continuous practice and review.

C

Improve Market Analysis

Continue developing the ability to study market structure, price behavior, fundamentals, liquidity, and news context.

D

Protect Capital

Make risk management a permanent part of the trading process and prioritize survival over aggressive growth.

E

Grow Through Experience

Use real observations, mistakes, reviews, and accumulated practice to improve decision-making over time.

F

Keep Learning

Continue studying and adapting as knowledge, experience, and understanding of the financial markets develop.

03 — Development Roadmap

How I Want to Grow

A simple progression for turning study into disciplined practice.

STEP 01

Learn

Study concepts, market behavior, risk, psychology, and news awareness.

STEP 02

Practice

Apply what I learn through chart study, observation, and structured practice.

STEP 03

Record

Keep a journal of decisions, mistakes, results, and lessons.

STEP 04

Review

Identify what needs improvement and refine the process with patience.

04 — My Trading Vision

Knowledge First. Discipline Always.

My vision is to build myself into a disciplined market participant through knowledge, patience, risk management, and continuous improvement. I do not want to measure progress only by profit. I also want to measure it by how well I follow my plan, control risk, learn from mistakes, and stay consistent.

✓ Follow the trading plan
✓ Avoid emotional decisions
✓ Protect capital first
✓ Quality over quantity
✓ Review mistakes regularly
✓ Stay patient and controlled
