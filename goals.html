Trading Goals

My goals are focused on learning, discipline, consistency and long-term development rather than chasing quick profits.

My Main Goals

I am building my trading journey around realistic and process-focused goals.

01

Build Strong Knowledge

Develop a deeper understanding of Forex markets, price action, technical analysis and fundamental factors.

02

Develop Discipline

Build the patience and discipline required to follow a structured trading process without unnecessary emotional decisions.

03

Master Risk Management

Understand position sizing, risk control and capital protection as essential parts of responsible trading.

04

Improve Market Analysis

Become better at analyzing market structure, price behavior, important levels and potential trading opportunities.

05

Build a Trading Plan

Develop clear rules for analysis, entries, risk management, trade management and review.

06

Maintain a Trading Journal

Record observations, decisions, mistakes and lessons to identify patterns and improve the trading process over time.

07

Develop Consistency

Focus on consistent execution of a process instead of measuring progress only through individual trade results.

08

Learn From Mistakes

Treat mistakes and losing trades as opportunities to review, understand and improve the decision-making process.

09

Think Long Term

Build knowledge and experience gradually with a long-term mindset instead of looking for shortcuts.

Long-Term Vision

The goal is to become a more knowledgeable, disciplined and responsible market participant over time.

Knowledge → Experience → Discipline → Consistency

My long-term vision is to continue learning, gain experience through practice and develop a disciplined approach to the financial markets. I do not want to rush the process. I want to build my skills properly and allow experience to develop over time.

What I Want to Build

These principles are more important to me than short-term results.

Knowledge Before Confidence

I want my confidence to come from understanding and preparation, not from a few successful trades.

Process Before Profit

The quality of the decision-making process matters more than chasing a particular profit target.

Risk Before Reward

Understanding and controlling risk should come before thinking about potential returns.

Patience Before Action

Not every market movement needs a trade. Waiting for a suitable setup is part of learning discipline.

My Goal Mindset

“The goal is not to become successful quickly. The goal is to become capable, disciplined and consistent over time.”

— Mahadin Trader
