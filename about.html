My Story

Still learning. Still building.

My Forex journey began on January 28, 2024. Since then, my focus has been on understanding the market rather than pretending to have mastered it.

I’m Mahmudul Hasan, the creator of Mahadin Trader. I’m currently learning Forex trading and building my knowledge step by step through study, market analysis, practice, and experience.

This website is a personal record of that journey — a place to document what I’m learning, reflect on mistakes, improve my process, and grow toward becoming a more disciplined trader.

My goal is not to rush for profits. I want to develop the knowledge, patience, risk awareness and mindset required for long-term growth in the financial markets.

“The goal is progress, not perfection.”
