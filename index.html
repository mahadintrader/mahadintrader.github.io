My Process

Process before profit.

I believe long-term trading development starts with a strong process rather than chasing quick results.

01

Study

Learn concepts and understand why the market may behave in different ways.

02

Analyze

Review charts, market structure, levels, news, and possible scenarios before making decisions.

03

Practice

Apply knowledge through structured practice and observation instead of rushing into decisions.

04

Journal

Record decisions, mistakes, emotions, lessons, and observations for future review.

05

Review

Look back at previous decisions to identify weaknesses and areas that need improvement.

06

Improve

Build better habits, stronger discipline, and a more consistent trading process over time.
