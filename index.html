02 / PROCESS

The Trading Framework.

A repeatable framework designed to keep decision-making structured and emotion under control.

01
◈

Core Discipline

The foundation of consistent decision-making.

  • No revenge trading
  • Follow the trading plan
  • No emotional decisions
  • Avoid overtrading
02
◇

Risk Management

Capital protection comes before profit.

  • Target risk: 1–2% per trade
  • Daily loss limit: 3–5%
  • Stop after 2–3 consecutive losses
  • Always define Stop Loss
03
◌

Trading Plan

No setup, no trade.

  • Setup confirmed
  • Trend verified
  • Minimum R:R target 1:2
  • News impact checked
04
◎

Economic Awareness

Understand the environment before entering.

  • Check economic calendar
  • High-impact news awareness
  • Avoid unnecessary exposure
05
◫

Performance Review

Every trade is data for improvement.

  • Track decisions
  • Record mistakes
  • Review losses
  • Improve weekly
06
◉

Mindset

Consistency is built through controlled behavior.

  • Discipline over emotion
  • Losses are part of learning
  • Stay patient
