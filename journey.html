The Journey So Far

A personal timeline of my progress, learning, practice, and development in Forex trading.

January 28, 2024

The Journey Begins

My Forex trading journey started on January 28, 2024. I began exploring the financial markets and developing an understanding of how Forex trading works.

Learning Phase

Building the Foundation

I started studying different areas of trading, including Price Action, Technical Analysis, Fundamental Analysis, Support and Resistance, Supply and Demand, and Candlestick Analysis.

Developing Knowledge

Exploring Trading Concepts

As my learning continued, I explored concepts such as Smart Money Concept (SMC), ICT, market structure, risk management, trading psychology, and economic news awareness.

Practice & Review

Learning Through Practice

I continue to study charts, analyze market conditions, practice different concepts, review mistakes, and develop a better understanding of my own trading process.

Present

Still Learning

My journey is still ongoing. I am not presenting myself as an expert. I am continuing to learn, practice, improve my discipline, and build the mindset required for long-term development.

My Learning Process

I believe consistent development comes from a structured learning and review process.

01

Study

Learn trading concepts, market behavior, technical analysis, fundamentals, and risk management.

02

Analyze

Study charts and market conditions to understand possible setups and price behavior.

03

Practice

Apply what I learn through chart study, backtesting, and practical observation.

04

Journal

Record observations, decisions, mistakes, lessons, and important trading experiences.

05

Review

Look back at previous work to identify mistakes, weaknesses, strengths, and areas for improvement.

06

Improve

Use lessons from study and review to gradually improve knowledge, discipline, patience, and consistency.

“The goal is not to rush for profits. The goal is to build the knowledge, discipline, patience, and mindset needed for long-term growth.”
