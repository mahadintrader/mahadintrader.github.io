My Forex Trading Journey

A personal journey of learning Forex trading through study, market analysis, practice, journaling, discipline, and continuous improvement.

لا إله إلا الله محمد رسول الله

My Forex trading journey started on January 28, 2024. Since then, I have been learning step by step and building my understanding of the financial markets through education, practice, observation, and experience.

Trading Journey

From Beginning to Present

Every stage of the journey is part of the learning process.

January 28, 2024

The Journey Begins

My Forex trading journey officially began. I started exploring the financial markets and developing an interest in understanding how Forex works.

Learning Phase

Building the Foundation

I began studying the basic concepts of Forex trading, including charts, price movement, market structure, support and resistance, and risk awareness.

Developing Knowledge

Exploring Trading Concepts

I continued learning different approaches including Price Action, Technical Analysis, Fundamental Analysis, Supply & Demand, Smart Money Concepts, and ICT concepts.

Practice & Review

Learning Through Practice

I started focusing more on market observation, practice, reviewing decisions, understanding mistakes, and developing better trading habits.

Present

Still Learning

I am still learning and improving. My focus remains on knowledge, discipline, risk management, patience, and becoming a more consistent and responsible trader over time.

My Learning Process

Study. Analyze. Practice. Improve.

I believe trading development is a continuous process.

01

Study

Learn trading concepts, market behavior, risk management, and trading psychology.

02

Analyze

Observe charts and study price movement, structure, levels, and market conditions.

03

Practice

Apply what I learn through structured practice and careful market observation.

04

Journal

Record observations, ideas, decisions, emotions, mistakes, and lessons.

05

Review

Review previous decisions and identify what worked, what did not, and what I learned.

06

Improve

Use lessons from previous experience to improve knowledge, discipline, and consistency.

Trading Mindset

“I want to measure progress not only by results, but by how well I follow my plan, control risk, learn from mistakes, and stay consistent. ”
