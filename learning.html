Learning & Skills

A structured look at the concepts, skills and knowledge I am studying as I continue my Forex trading journey.

My Learning Focus

These are the main areas I am currently studying and practicing to build a stronger foundation in Forex trading.

01

Price Action

Studying how price moves and learning to understand market behavior through raw price movement.

02

Technical Analysis

Learning charts, market structure, trends, levels and other technical tools used for market analysis.

03

Fundamental Analysis

Understanding economic conditions, major events and fundamental factors that can influence currency markets.

04

Supply & Demand

Studying supply and demand zones and how price may react around important areas of the market.

05

Smart Money Concept

Exploring SMC concepts to better understand liquidity, market structure and institutional-style price behavior.

06

ICT Concepts

Learning ICT-based market concepts and studying how they can be applied to market analysis.

07

Support & Resistance

Practicing the identification of important price levels and understanding possible reactions around those levels.

08

Candlestick Analysis

Studying candlestick patterns and price behavior to improve chart reading and market understanding.

09

Risk Management

Learning how proper risk control, position sizing and discipline can help protect trading capital.

10

Trading Psychology

Developing awareness of emotions, patience, discipline and decision-making during the trading process.

11

Trading Plan

Learning how to create structured rules and conditions for analyzing and managing trades.

12

Economic & News Awareness

Following important economic events and understanding why major news can affect the Forex market.

How I Learn

My focus is on gradual improvement rather than trying to master everything at once.

Study → Analyze → Practice → Journal → Review → Improve

I am building my trading knowledge step by step through study, chart analysis, practice and regular review. The goal is to understand what I am doing, recognize mistakes and continuously improve my process.

Learning Roadmap

A simple framework for developing knowledge and discipline over time.

01 — Foundation Learn Forex basics, terminology and market structure.
02 — Analysis Study technical and fundamental approaches.
03 — Practice Practice analysis and review historical market behavior.
04 — Discipline Develop risk management, psychology and consistent habits.

Learning Mindset

“I am not trying to become a trader overnight. I am building the knowledge and discipline one step at a time.”

— Mahadin Trader
