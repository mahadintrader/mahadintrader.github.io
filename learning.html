My Learning Approach

A simple process I want to follow throughout the journey.

01 — Study

Learn a concept carefully and build a clear foundation before trying to apply it.

02 — Practice

Observe charts, analyze setups and practice applying the concepts in a controlled way.

03 — Review

Review decisions, identify mistakes and use the lessons to improve future practice.
