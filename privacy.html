Privacy at Mahadin Trader

Mahadin Trader respects your privacy. This website is primarily a personal Forex learning and educational website. This Privacy Policy explains the general approach to information that may be shared with or collected through the website.

1. Information You Provide

If you contact Mahadin Trader by email or through social media, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, message, or other information you choose to share.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact messages.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information you voluntarily provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your questions or messages.
  • Communicate with you when necessary.
  • Understand feedback about the website.
  • Improve the website and its educational content.

3. Email Communication

If you contact Mahadin Trader by email, your email address and message may be retained as part of the normal communication process.

Your information will not be intentionally published on this website without your permission.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may not directly use cookies for personal tracking. However, third-party services, hosting platforms, analytics tools, embedded content, or external websites may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Third-Party Websites

Mahadin Trader may provide links to external websites and social media platforms such as Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Messenger, or other resources.

Once you leave this website and visit a third-party service, that service's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

6. Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, or server logs may be handled by the hosting provider according to its own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information through the website.

8. Data Security

Reasonable care is taken to protect information shared directly with Mahadin Trader. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website develops or as its features change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Your Choices

You are free to decide whether to contact Mahadin Trader or provide personal information. You can also choose not to use external links or third-party services provided on the website.
